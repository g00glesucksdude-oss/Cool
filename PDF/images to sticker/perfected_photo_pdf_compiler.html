Ag Sample
Scans 3 photos at once (worker pool) and skips full-page layout analysis in favor of a faster scattered-text mode, since a photo is closer to "labels here and there" than a printed page. Ignores speck-sized detections, needs at least 3 confident text detections before trusting them, and looks at the typical (not literal min/max) word size, so one stray misread can't force a runaway resize. If a photo genuinely needs enlarging to stay readable, it may get turned 90° first (never mirrored) if that lets it fit using less page height — and one photo's boost is always capped well under a full page, so your images-per-page target can't collapse to just one or two photos. Cached per photo in this browser, so recompiling or resuming after an interruption reuses scans instead of redoing them. If a photo's small text isn't important to you, just pin its size manually in the page preview after compiling — a manual pin always wins.
Last resort: when a page has leftover blank space no whole photo fits into, slices the next eligible photo with a straight cut — one piece fills the gap, the rest carries to the next page — so the pieces can be cut and taped back together. Skips exact-size photos and anything with detected text.

3. Compile

Tap any photo in a preview above to pin its size — a photo that already fits beside it will fill the gutter instead of leaving it blank, and pinning never shrinks anyone else. Then "Export PDF" to download the updated result. Only the page(s) actually affected by your edit get re-rendered, so pinning stays fast even with hundreds of photos. "Check Readability" re-checks text against the sizes photos are actually printing at right now (after any resizing you've done) and highlights anything that's gotten too small — use ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys to step through flagged photos. Photos are decoded and released one at a time (or one page at a time) rather than all held in memory together.
