## **1. Modern Biotech & GMOs**

### **Core Definitions**

* **Modern Biotechnology:** Using DNA, cells, or microbes to change an organism's traits.
* **GMO (Genetically Modified Organism):** An organism whose DNA was altered in a lab to add or boost traits (e.g., higher nutrition, pest resistance).

---

## **2. Lab Techniques**

### **DNA Fingerprinting**

* **Purpose:** Identifies individuals using unique DNA patterns.
* **Key Mechanism:** Uses **polymorphic markers** (unique regions in DNA).
* **Sample Types:** Blood, saliva, urine, hair follicles, semen.

### **PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)**

* **Nickname:** **"Molecular Photocopying"**
* **Function:** Rapidly creates millions/billions of copies of a DNA segment.

---

## **3. Gene Therapy**

> **Concept:** Fixes faulty genes to treat diseases without major surgery.

* **In Vivo:** Gene injected **directly inside** the body.
* **Ex Vivo:** Cells removed $\rightarrow$ modified in lab $\rightarrow$ **returned** to body.
* **Target Conditions:**
* **Hemophilia:** Blood can't clot properly.
* **Cystic Fibrosis:** Mucus buildup damages lungs/breathing.
* **Sickle Cell Anemia:** Misshapen (sickle) red blood cells block blood flow.



---

## **4. Reproductive Tech**

* **IVF (In Vitro Fertilization):**
1. Sperm + Egg fertilized in a lab dish $\rightarrow$ **Zygote**.
2. Zygote grows $\rightarrow$ **Embryo**.
3. Embryo implanted in the **uterus**.


* **ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection):** A single sperm is injected **directly into** an egg.

## **5. Local Biotech & Crops (Philippines Focus)**

> 📌 **2003 Milestone:** The Philippines became the 1st country in its region to commercialize biotech crops.

### **Bt Crops (Bt Corn, Bt Eggplant, Bt Cotton)**

* **Bt (*Bacillus thuringiensis*):** A soil bacterium used as a natural source for pest-fighting genes.
* **Cry1Ac Gene:** Bacterial gene inserted into crops to produce a natural pest toxin.
* **Target Pests:** Corn borer & **EFSB** (Eggplant Fruit and Shoot Borer).

### **Golden Rice (Malusog Rice)**

* **Modification:** Engineered with corn and bacterial genes to make **Beta-carotene** (Vitamin A precursor).
* **4 Main Goals (Memory Trick: "4 Vitamin A Fixes"):**
1. Fight Vitamin A deficiency.
2. Reduce child deaths.
3. Prevent blindness.
4. Boost immune system.
