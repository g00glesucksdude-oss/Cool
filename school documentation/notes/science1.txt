## **Modern Biotechnology & Genetic Engineering**

### **Core Definition**

* **Modern Biotechnology:** The manipulation of genetic material (DNA, genes, cells, and microorganisms) using advanced techniques to alter an organism's traits, commonly referred to as genetic engineering.



### **Key Techniques & Processes**

* **DNA Fingerprinting:**
* Used to identify individuals based on their unique DNA sequences.


* While organisms of the same species share similar general sequences, individual uniqueness is determined by **polymorphic markers**.


* Common biological samples collected for testing include blood, saliva, urine, hair follicles, and semen.




* **Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR):** Also known as **molecular photocopying**, a laboratory technique used to generate millions or billions of copies of a specific DNA segment.


* **Gene Therapy:** A treatment method for genetic diseases designed to avoid major medical procedures or surgery by inserting genetic material into a patient's cells.


* **In Vivo Gene Therapy:** Genetic material is packaged and injected *directly* into the patient's body without removing cells.


* **Ex Vivo Gene Therapy:** Cells are removed from the patient's body, altered in a laboratory setting, and then returned.





### **Reproductive Technologies**

* **In Vitro Fertilization (IVF):** A technique where an egg cell and a sperm cell are fertilized outside the body in a controlled laboratory Petri dish.


* The fertilized egg becomes a **zygote**, which develops into an **embryo**.


* The embryo is subsequently transferred and implanted into the female's uterus.




* **Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI):** A specialized form of IVF where a single sperm cell is injected directly into an egg cell.



### **Local Applications & Examples in the Philippines**

* Mentioned Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs) developed or utilized in the country include corn, eggplants (*talong*), and rice.
